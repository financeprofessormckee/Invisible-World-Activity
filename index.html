Friedman Economic Games · Proposal 5

The Invisible World

In the real world you never see a supply curve or a demand curve, only the price and quantity where they cross. Change the conditions, watch where the market lands, and work out both hidden curves.

How to play
  1. Point A shows the market price and quantity under the starting conditions. The curves that produced it are invisible.
  2. Change any of the three conditions and choose Observe to see where the market lands next. Each observation after A costs 5 points; you may take up to 12.
  3. When you think you know a curve, choose Supply or Demand, pick the observation whose conditions it applies to, and give the line: through two observations, by typing two endpoints, or by clicking two spots on the graph.
  4. A wrong curve costs 10 points. Find both curves to keep your full score; finding only one halves it.
  5. When you finish, the replay reveals the true curves behind every point.
Hint

Every observed point sits on both curves at once. If you change something that moves only supply, demand stays put, so the new point and the old one lie on the same demand curve. Which condition can you trust to move only one curve? Is the third one safe to assume? Moving a slider several steps at once makes a bigger, easier-to-read jump.
